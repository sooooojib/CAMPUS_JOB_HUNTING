# UniCareer Community — Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

**UniCareer Community** is a high-trust, university-connected career ecosystem designed to unify university students, alumni, faculty, staff, and authorized industry partners. Unlike open, impersonal job boards or single-purpose campus portals, UniCareer blends verified institutional affiliation with modern community networking, mentorship, career tools, and opportunity distribution.

## 2. Core Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose & Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Curated Opportunity Portal** | Structured distribution of internships, full-time jobs, and part-time roles with clear distinction between official employer postings and community-shared referrals. |
| **2. Professional Community** | An authenticated feed for career discussions, advice, industry insights, milestone sharing, and peer encouragement. |
| **3. High-Trust Referral Network** | Facilitating transparent referral requests where verified alumni and campus connections can advocate for students and peers. |
| **4. Alumni & Member Directory** | Searchable networking directory filterable by graduation year, field of study, current employer, industry, and willingness to mentor. |
| **5. Student Career Toolkit** | Tailored tools for verified students: CGPA eligibility checking, skill gap analysis, personalized job matching, resume tracking, and career readiness milestones. |

## 3. Target Audience & Stakeholders

- **Current Students**: Seeking internships, entry-level roles, alumni mentorship, and career readiness guidance.
- **Alumni**: Seeking mid-to-senior opportunities, hiring for their teams, mentoring younger peers, and reconnecting with campus cohorts.
- **Faculty & University Staff**: Monitoring student placement, sharing academic/research opportunities, and validating institutional records.
- **Vetted Employers & Recruiters**: Connecting directly with verified university talent through official postings and talent pipeline engagement.
- **Approved External Connections**: Industry leaders, guest lecturers, or affiliates connected to institutional initiatives, approved via admin review.

## 4. Key Differentiators

- **Gated Trust Model**: Mandatory authentication and verified affiliation eliminate spam, fake recruiters, and anonymous trolling.
- **Dual-Role Single Account**: A user can apply for jobs while simultaneously posting opportunities or referrals when authorized — no separate recruiter login required.
- **Transparent Provenance**: Clear labeling of `OFFICIAL_EMPLOYER` vs. `COMMUNITY_SHARED` listings ensures applicants always know who is behind an opportunity.
- **Admin Review Gate**: All new opportunities are reviewed before becoming visible, guaranteeing high-signal content.
