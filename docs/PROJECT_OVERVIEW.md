# UniCareer Community — Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

**UniCareer Community** is a high-trust, university-connected career and networking ecosystem uniting **Current Students**, **Alumni**, and **University Administrators** within the institutional community.

Unlike public, impersonal job boards or single-purpose campus portals, UniCareer blends verified university affiliation with modern professional networking, mentorship, and peer-advocated opportunity distribution.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose & Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Curated Opportunity Portal** | Structured distribution of community-shared internships and full-time jobs with verified company profiles and external portal links. |
| **2. Professional Community Feed** | An authenticated campus feed for career discussions, advice, industry insights, and peer milestone sharing. |
| **3. High-Trust Referral Network** | Direct referral requests allowing verified alumni to advocate for students at their respective employers. |
| **4. Member Network Directory** | Searchable institutional directory filterable by role, department, current company, skills, and mentorship availability. |
| **5. Institutional Governance Center** | Administrative command console for credential verification review, CGPA verification, opportunity moderation, and security auditing. |

---

## 3. The Three Primary Personas

1. **Current Students (`STUDENT`)**: Seeking internships, entry-level roles, alumni mentorship, and community-shared opportunities.
2. **Alumni (`ALUMNI`)**: Sharing job opportunities, providing internal referrals for students at their companies, and mentoring university peers.
3. **Platform Administrators (`ADMIN`)**: University administrators and governance staff reviewing credential verifications, verifying student CGPAs, moderating opportunities, and maintaining platform safety.

---

## 4. Key Differentiators

- **Gated Institutional Trust Model**: Two-stage verification gate — mandatory email confirmation followed by administrator dossier approval. Unapproved users cannot log in until an administrator approves their credentials and dispatches an official approval email.
- **Dual-Role Unified Account**: A user can apply for jobs while simultaneously posting opportunities or referrals when authorized — no separate employer login required.
- **Explainable Matching (Zero Blackbox AI)**: Skill match scores use a transparent formula: $\frac{\text{Matched Required Skills}}{\text{Total Required Skills}} \times 100$, paired with strict eligibility criteria (Department, CGPA, Deadline).
- **Authentic External Applications**: Direct links to official company hiring portals ("Apply on Official Website"), keeping candidate applications authentic, direct, and spam-free without file uploads.
- **Admin Review Gate**: All new opportunities are reviewed before becoming visible, guaranteeing high-signal, compliant postings.
